Overnight Oats

Home

Description

Overnight oats are easy to make, easy to eat, and super healthy.

Ingredients

  • Milk
  • Instant or Quick oats
  • Brown sugar
  • Chia seeds
  • Powdered Cinnamon

Toppings

  • Banana slices
  • Peanut butter
  • Chocolate chips

Steps

  1. Into a 12oz mason jar, mix 1/2cup milk, 1/2cup oats, 2tbsp brown sugar, 1tbsp chia seeds, and 1 tsp powdered cinnamon
  2. Refrigerate overnight to give the oats time to absorb the milk.
  3. Add toppings and enjoy
