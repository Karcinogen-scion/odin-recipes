Raspberry Vanilla Kombucha

Home

Kombucha is a super healthy probiotic drink. It's easy to make, but requires some dedication.

Ingredients

  • Loose leaf black tea
  • Pure granulated cane sugar
  • Water
  • Raspberries
  • Vanilla extract

Steps

  1. First, brew about 3/4G of extra sweet tea. Let it cool to less than 100 degrees before moving on.
  2. Add your tea to your 1/4G of existing kombucha culture.
  3. Ferment for four days
  4. On the fourth day, blend your raspberries and pour them into the kombucha. Add your vanilla extract. Mix well
  5. On the seventh day, strain and pour your kombucha into sealed bottles and refrigerate.
  6. After 24h in the fridge, your kombucha is ready to drink
