Poor Man's Chipotle

Home

Description

This is an excellent recipe for meal prepping. It's easy to make, reheats well, super healthy, and on top of all that, is super affordable.

Ingredients

  • Jasmine rice
  • Black beans
  • Pinto beans
  • Corn
  • Chipotle Seasoning
  • Olive Oil
  • Salt (optional)
  • Bell peppers (red,yellow,orange)

Toppings

  • Cilantro
  • Sour cream
  • Valentina hot sauce
  • Shredded cheese

Steps

  1. Use your pressure cooker to cook the jasmine rice. Two cups rice, two cups water, 2 tbsp olive oil
  2. When your rice is done. Move it into a reusable container
  3. Pour 1 can corn, 1 can black beans, and 1 can pinto beans into a skillet. Seasoning with 2 tbsp chipotle seasoning
  4. When your corn and beans are done cooking. Move them into a reusable container
  5. Cut your bell peppers into thin strips. I usually use 3 bell pepper (one of each color)
  6. Cook your bell peppers on your skillet. Drizzle plenty of olive oil and cook on high
  7. Portion the Ingredients in a bowl and your ready to serve. I recommend storing their ingredients separately and combining them when you're ready to eat.
  8. Tie it all together with your desired toppings
